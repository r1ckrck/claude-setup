# Concept — spec

**Purpose.** The novel organizing idea the whole site is built on — the keystone. It is what makes
the portfolio unmistakably this person's rather than a template. It **gates** several later
decisions: color and canvas character, light/dark, layout, and the information architecture. It is
often the biggest creative task and may be **deferred** to a dedicated session; if so, record
candidate directions as seeds and mark it deferred so nothing downstream is blocked.

Brand sets the attributes, personality, and art direction; the concept is the idea that expresses
them as an experience.

## Interview

### [OPEN] The one idea
> "If the site had a single organizing idea — something a visitor could feel — what would it be?
> It should come from who you are and what you make, not from a trend."

### [OPEN] Brainstorm directions
> "Generate several directions. Strong seeds tie to the throughline or the work itself — an idea
> that enacts your story, a space that holds your range, a system the visitor operates. Cast wide,
> then cut."

### [CHOICE] Pick a direction
Offer the generated directions as options — use previews to sketch each. Recommend the one that
(a) is most unmistakably theirs, (b) reads as novel *as an interaction*, not just a theme, and
(c) the build can actually carry. Always allow "keep exploring."

If the concept needs more time, **defer it**: write `> Deferred — <what's needed>` and record the
candidate directions as seeds under Direction. Do not block the rest of the foundation on it.

## Outline
1. **Direction** — the chosen concept (or candidate seeds if deferred).
2. **How it works** — the experience/interaction, in a few lines.
3. **Why it fits** — ties to brand, positioning, and narrative.
4. **What it gates** — color & canvas, light/dark, layout, IA — the decisions that wait on this.
5. **Status** — chosen, or deferred with the marker.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Novel and unmistakably this person's — not a template, not a trend.
- [ ] Reads as a concept you could *experience*, not just a label.
- [ ] Ties explicitly to the brand, positioning, and narrative.
- [ ] Names what it gates (color / light-dark / layout / IA).
- [ ] If deferred, the marker is present and candidate directions are recorded as seeds.
